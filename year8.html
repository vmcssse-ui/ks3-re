Week 1w/c 7 Sep 2026Lesson

Prejudice and Discrimination #w1

Sets the ground rules for RE and opens the year's first big idea: what prejudice and discrimination actually mean. Students separate a thought from an action, define key terms and agree how the class will handle sensitive discussion.

▶Expectations PPT▶Prejudice And Discrimination◔Bell Work - Definitions⚑Lesson Plan - Prejudice And Discrimination
Week 2w/c 14 Sep 2026Lesson

Judging by Appearances #w2

Why we judge people on how they look, and how often those snap judgements are wrong. Students test their own first impressions against evidence, then examine where appearance-based assumptions come from and what they cost.

▶Judging By Appearances◔Bell Work - Statements⚑Differentiation - Judging By Appearances⚑Lesson Plan - Judging By Appearances
Week 3w/c 21 Sep 2026Lesson

What is Racism? #w3

Builds a working definition of racism, distinguishing individual prejudice from wider structures. Students look at historical and present-day examples and practise explaining why racism is both a moral and a legal issue.

▶What Is Racism⚑What Is Racism - Lesson Plan
Week 4w/c 28 Sep 2026Formative

Formative Assessment #w4

First formative assessment of the year. Students write under timed conditions on prejudice, appearance and racism, then use the DIT lesson to read the mark scheme, correct their own work and set a target.

📘Unit 1 Booklet✎Formative✎New Formative↻DIT - Formative Assessment
Week 5w/c 5 Oct 2026Lesson

Challenging Racism #w5

Moves from describing racism to challenging it. Students study bystander behaviour and real campaigns, then plan a practical response for their own school setting, judging which approaches actually change minds.

▶Challenging Racism⚑Lesson Plan - Challenging Racism
Week 6w/c 12 Oct 2026Lesson

Xenophobia #w6

Xenophobia — fear and hostility towards people seen as foreign. Students trace how migration is described in the media, separate fact from rhetoric, and compare xenophobia with the racism studied earlier.

▶Xenophobia⚑Lesson Plan - Xenophobia
Week 7w/c 19 Oct 2026Lesson

Gender #w7

Gender prejudice and stereotyping. Students examine expectations placed on boys and girls, how those expectations narrow choices, and what equal treatment looks like in school, work and religious communities.

▶Gender⚑Lesson Plan - Gender
